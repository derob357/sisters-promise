Handcrafted in small batches, this soap is designed to support healthy, balanced skin while leaving it soft, calm, and refreshed. Formulated with organic oils and botanicals, Sea Moss + Aloe Soap creates a creamy, gentle lather that cleanses without stripping the skin's natural moisture. The fresh, clean aroma offers a calming aromatherapy experience, making it ideal for daily use.
